Babel

Ten signs, and a hand for a word. Not a sign language — a demonstration of the recognition and the avatar.

about

These ten "signs" are synthetic handshapes, drawn geometrically — not a recording of a real signer and not a claim about any sign language. There is no camera here: you pick a reference hand, or a noisy one, and the app recognises it or refuses to.

Recognise a hand

Pick a reference hand to see it recognised, or a noisy one to see it refused rather than guessed.

Type a word

The avatar is drawn from that word's stored reference. A word outside the ten has no hand, and the app says so.

The ten

Babel recognises a hand from ten synthetic handshapes and draws the stored handshape for a word. A hand outside the vocabulary returns "not recognised" rather than a guess, and everything runs on this device.
