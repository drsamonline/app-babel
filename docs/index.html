Babel

Ten signs, and a hand for a word. A hand outside the vocabulary is refused rather than guessed.

Open Babel

These ten “signs” are synthetic handshapes, drawn geometrically — not a recording of a real signer and not a claim about any sign language. There is no camera and no trained model in this app: you pick a reference hand or a noisy one, and it recognises it or refuses to.

Status Built and working: ten reference handshapes of 21 points; a scale-normalised distance; recognition that returns the nearest word or “not recognised”; and an avatar drawn from a word's stored reference.
Release: Windows EXE, Android APK and Linux binary, built from this source in CI — app-latest.
Not built: a camera, and a trained sign-language model. Both are real work, and a demo that pretended to have them would be the worst kind of fake.

hello · no · yes · water · eat · stop · more · help · sorry · thank you

What it does

  • Compares a hand of 21 points against ten reference handshapes, scale-normalised so a big hand and a small hand compare.
  • Returns the nearest word — or “not recognised” when nothing is close enough.
  • Draws an avatar from a word's stored handshape, and refuses a word outside the ten.

What it will not do

  • Guess. Beyond the threshold it says “not recognised”, because a wrong word is worse than no word.
  • Invent a hand for a word it does not have.
  • Claim to be a sign language. The signs are synthetic, and the page says so above the fold.
  • Use a camera. There is none: getUserMedia appears nowhere.
  • Use the internet. No fetch, no endpoint; CSP connect-src 'none'.

When things go wrong

CaseWhat happens
Bad inputA hand with the wrong number of points, or no hand, is refused; an empty word is refused.
Outside the vocabulary“not recognised”, with the nearest reference and its distance named — and no word offered.
No networkNothing changes; everything is local.
Storage full or unavailableNOT SAVED, and the history on screen is unchanged.
Corrupt saved stateReported and offered for export; never replaced with an empty one.
