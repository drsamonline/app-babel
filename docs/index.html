Plate I · handshapes · ten specimens

Babel — a field guide

Ten handshapes, drawn geometrically. A hand that is not one of them is refused, not guessed.

What it does

Compares a hand of twenty-one points against ten reference sketches, scale-normalised so a big hand and a small one compare. It answers with the nearest word — or with not recognised.

And back again

Type a word and it draws that word's stored handshape, point for point.

What it refuses

Beyond the threshold it offers no word at all. A small vocabulary that guesses is worse than one that admits its limits — a wrong caption on a hand is a wrong sentence about a person.

These ten signs are synthetic. They are not a recording of a real signer, not taken from any sign language, and not a claim about how any Deaf community signs anything. No camera, no trained model.
